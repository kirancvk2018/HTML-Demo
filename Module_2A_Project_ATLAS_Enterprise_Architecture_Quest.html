Project ATLAS: The Enterprise Architecture Quest

A client has asked Ness to turn a promising agentic prototype into an enterprise solution. Your decisions will shape the architecture. Each round adds new information, raises the difficulty and tests whether you can think like an enterprise architect.

Your mission

GlobalTech Manufacturing

GlobalTech has approximately 8,000 software engineers. Its engineering knowledge is spread across repositories, tickets, documents and specialist systems. The company wants Project ATLAS to help engineers analyse change requests, understand code impact, propose tests, review risks and produce a recommendation for human review.

Your role is to advise GlobalTech on the enterprise solution architecture—not just the agent design.

What the client expects

  • Translate business needs into architecture requirements.
  • Position Claude and agents within existing systems.
  • Define layers, boundaries, dependencies and data flows.
  • Choose suitable architecture patterns.
  • Defend build-versus-integrate decisions and trade-offs.
Important: The case gives no numeric availability, latency or cost targets. A good architect records unknowns instead of inventing targets.
