📊 Process Overview

The CFO Coordinator orchestrates an intelligent financial analysis workflow. Three specialized analyzers (Finance KPI, Finance Email, and Trend Analysis) work in parallel to gather critical data. These insights converge at the Risk Detection Skill, which synthesizes the analysis. The workflow then proceeds through a serial pipeline of specialized writers and publishers, ultimately delivering comprehensive financial reports and recommendations via email to stakeholders.

🎯 Coordinator: Initiates workflow (instant)
⚡ Parallel Phase: 3 analyzers work simultaneously (2-3 sec)
🔀 Risk Detection: Consolidates all inputs (1 sec)
✍️ Report Writing: Generates recommendations (2 sec)
💾 Storage: Publishes to Google Drive (1 sec)
📧 Distribution: Sends via email (instant)
