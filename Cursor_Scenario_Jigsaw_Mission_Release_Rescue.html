MISSION: RELEASE RESCUE

A scenario-based Cursor AI jigsaw challenge. A device diagnostics release is failing, the deadline is close, and the engineering team has one mission: investigate, fix, test, review, and prepare credible release evidence without losing control of the code.

Incident brief: Project Atlas

It is 2:15 PM. The release candidate for a fictional connected-device diagnostics service has started returning intermittent errors. The team has three clues: a recent multi-file change, inconsistent test results, and a log showing repeated timeouts. The release review begins in 90 minutes.

Your team has Cursor IDE and Cursor Agent. You must assemble the 12-step engineering response in the right order. Every tile is an action, tool choice, or control drawn from the two-day Cursor training themes.

Constraint: Do not let AI “just fix everything.” Keep changes bounded, verify evidence, protect sensitive information, and require a human approval before release.
